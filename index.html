Built in the open, one piece at a time
A working project.
An open invitation.
Built by Nimit Mehra out of a personal research practice spanning Indian equities, US equities, crypto and macro. The aim is to make that work useful—and adaptable—to other people and their agents.
Four source-linked seed packs and a workspace initializer are open source. Start with these focused maps, or ask us to help seed a graph around your own universe. Full private tracker releases remain separate.
Share a use case ↗Get in touch →
Can I run this today?
Yes. Download a source-linked seed for any of the four lenses. The initializer creates a local graph, configuration, source-review queue and update guide. It does not fetch live data, call a model or schedule itself. The older fictional demo is retained separately.
Will this give me earlier signals?
The goal is to notice relevant changes and overlooked connections sooner in your research process. Whether a signal is useful must be tested. No predictive accuracy, investment return or timing advantage is promised.
Can I use my own graph and scoring rules?
Yes. Start with a whole pack or an entity and its neighboring relationships, then keep your own research in your repository. The seeds include questions to investigate. Production source connectors, scoring, change detection and scheduling still need implementation.
Is this a financial data API?
No. There is no hosted data API, managed scheduling service or live MCP endpoint here. Public research artifacts and demo files have different scopes and should not be treated as a licensed, complete market dataset.
