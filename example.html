Our reading / For review

The June catch-up assumption needs revisiting.

The June catch-up assumption should be revisited: later management statements describe continuing constraints. These are changing expectations, not verified delivery outcomes.

What this changes

A research note that assumed the constraint would clear around June needs an update. Keep the earlier statement so the change remains visible.

What remains unanswered

How much revenue was delayed? When did capacity actually catch up? Which suppliers benefited? These three statements do not settle those questions.
